# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give useful feedback focused on the work.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants whose behavior harms the community.
